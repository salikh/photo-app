## What's wanted (as given)

Support video files in the library: recognize the various formats, and create two kinds of
thumbnails for them: static images (like Thumb/Small/Medium) and animated ones (gif/webm) in
similar sizes, holding a short excerpt, e.g. 5-10 one-second fragments cropped uniformly from the
original. Decided in a /grilling session 2026-10-08.

## Decisions

- **Decoder**: system `ffmpeg`/`ffprobe` via subprocess (not installed on this machine yet; deploy
  check + docs). Missing ffmpeg degrades to placeholders, never breaks indexing.
- **Animated format**: WebM (VP9, muted), shown in `<video muted loop>`; no GIF. Thumb 300 and
  Small 640 only; 8 x 1 s fragments from the centers of 8 equal slices (shorter than 8 s: whole).
  Medium/Huge are static only; the loupe plays the original.
- **Playback**: original via HTTP Range; unplayable codecs (AVI, WMV...) fall back to stills +
  download link. No transcoding (possible future ticket).
- **Model**: a video is a normal file/Photo, grouped by the usual stem rules; rating, tags, trash,
  move, XMP all unchanged. RAW tuning, crop, rotation don't apply. Implied tag `video`; no
  dedicated filter button (194 compares two cheap exposures and picks one).
- **Static thumbnails always show a play indicator** (triangle in a circle) for every video tile,
  also when the video can't be decoded or played. It is an **SVG overlay drawn by the client**
  (user, 2026-10-08), never burned into the JPEGs, so thumbnails stay clean frames.
- **Metadata**: duration, size (rotation-aware), codec, creation time (container, then filename
  timestamp, then mtime); no GPS.
- **Load**: ~770 videos (482 mp4, 176 mov, 108 avi, ...), populated in the background, one ffmpeg
  at a time, niced, per-video timeout, recorded failures. Only the static Thumb is made on demand.
- **Excluded**: AI rating and export skip videos.

## What's wanted

Grid shows the static thumb with an SVG play indicator overlay (white triangle in a translucent dark circle, centered, ~20% of the tile's short edge; one reusable inline SVG/CSS component, also used by the filmstrip and the loupe fallback; shown for every video, playable or not, and hidden while the animation plays) plus a duration badge. When an animated
thumb exists it plays (muted, looped) on mouse hover or keyboard focus (touch devices keep the still; the loupe plays the real video); never autoplays the whole grid (CPU, bandwidth). Falls back to the still when the
animation is missing. Filmstrip shows the still + duration.

Scan the whole `/zoo/Pictures` (847 GB, about 92k images) once into a real state directory, using
`--hashes_db` to avoid rehashing where possible. It takes a long time over the NAS (a directory of
800 files took about 15 minutes with hashing), so it is a background job and should be run by the
user or with the user's go-ahead. The scan only reads `/zoo/Pictures`; it writes only the state DB.

## Result of the whole-library scan (2026-09-21, 15:04-15:49)

Run as the per-year sequence of ticket [051](051.md): 35 steps, no errors, peak memory 130 MB.
`~/.local/share/photos` now holds **94,616 files in 66,352 Photos** (28,207 with several files),
15,447 sidecars and 46 tags. Only 392 files needed reading this time (the rest were read by the earlier
interrupted runs); most of the time went into a 35-minute walk of `Lightroom` (no image files).

- **Ratings** (7,229 Photos have at least one sidecar; resolved by newest-wins): 1 star 6,238 (86%),
  2 stars 424, 3 stars 206, 4 stars 7, 5 stars 5, reject 245, no rating 104. The 1-star mass is
  darktable's default, see [049](049.md) (`--one_star_is_unrated` hides it).
- **Orphan sidecars: 3,033** (20% of all sidecars) name a `.DNG` (1,649) or `.JPG` (1,383) that is not
  in the library at all, not even as a missing file or under another extension, plus one bare
  `2014/K___9911.XMP`. They look like leftovers of photos that were deleted after culling (for example
  `2018/2018-01-Yaita/K___0244.DNG.xmp`). The Attention page lists them. A reviewable clean-up script
  (the `cleanup.py` model) could remove them; not built, not requested.
- **Thumbnails** (files with a recorded thumbnail per size): Thumb 60,496 (2.6 GB), Small 60,564
  (5.2 GB), Medium 60,590 (10.9 GB), Huge 27,934 (28.8 GB). **Lacking:** Thumb 34,120 (36%), Small
  34,052, Medium 34,026 (35%), Huge 66,682 (70%). The gaps are whole directories: 2021 (8,848 files),
  2022 (6,490), `Exported` (4,114), 2024 (3,947), 2023 (3,542), 2025 (1,030), 2026 (1,062 of 1,096),
  `Receipts` (372), plus parts of 2018 (1,589 of 7,575) and 2019 (2,824 of 13,216). They are made on
  demand when a photo is opened (JPEG with Pillow, DNG from the embedded preview).

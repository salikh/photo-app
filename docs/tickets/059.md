# 059: Scrollable, tappable thumbnail strip below the main image

- Type: task
- Status: done
- Priority: later (user request 2026-09-21, "for later")
- Related: [027](027.md) Loupe view, [044](044.md) Phone layout, [045](045.md) Swipe navigation, [056](056.md) Photos leave the view, [050](050.md) Preloading
- Refs: `photoapp/static/loupe.js` (`renderFilmstrip`, `FILMSTRIP_RADIUS`), `photoapp/static/style.css` (`.filmstrip`)

Make the strip of thumbnails under the main image something you can **scroll** through the whole folder and
**click or tap** to jump to a photo.

## Today

- The strip shows only the current photo and 6 on each side (`FILMSTRIP_RADIUS = 6`, 13 thumbnails); it is
  rebuilt from scratch on every navigation, so a scroll position never survives and the strip can never
  show anything further away.
- Clicking one of those thumbnails jumps to it, but there is nothing else to click.
- It is **hidden on screens narrower than 700 px** (`display: none`), so on a phone there is no strip.
- The current photo is highlighted but the strip is not centered on it or scrolled to it.

## Target

- The strip covers the **whole list of the current folder and filter** and scrolls horizontally: mouse
  wheel and trackpad (vertical wheel maps to horizontal scrolling), drag/flick on touch, scrollbar,
  keyboard (Home/End keep working as today).
- **Click or tap a thumbnail to jump** to that photo (as now, for every thumbnail).
- The current photo is centered when the photo changes (smooth scroll, unless the user is scrolling by hand)
  and stays highlighted; scrolling the strip by hand does not change the photo until a thumbnail is chosen.
- Visible on phones too, as a slim strip (thumbnails at least 44 px high so they are tappable), without
  taking room the image needs; it must not conflict with the swipe gestures on the image (it sits outside
  the swipe area) or push the rating buttons off the screen.
- Works with the active filter, and stays correct when photos leave the list after a rating change ([056](056.md))
  and when they return on undo.

## Design points

- **Thousands of photos in a folder:** do not put thousands of `<img>` in the DOM. Virtualize (render only the
  thumbnails near the visible window with spacers for the rest) or use `loading="lazy"` with fixed-size
  placeholders. Decide after measuring a folder of several thousand.
- **Thumbnail requests:** only visible thumbnails are requested. A thumbnail of a RAW that has no preview yet
  triggers a background render on the server; scrolling fast through such a folder must not queue hundreds
  of jobs (cancel requests that scroll out of view, keep the existing retry for pictures still rendering).
- **Sizes:** the `Thumb` size (300 px) is enough for a 56 px strip; check that it is the one requested.
- **Interaction with preloading** ([050](050.md)): the strip's requests are low priority next to the
  neighbors' `Medium`/`Huge` preloads.
- Optional later: a rating badge (★, ✖) on the strip thumbnails, and dimming photos already handled.

## Result (2026-09-21)

`static/filmstrip.js` (new) replaces the fixed +-6 window; wired from `loupe.js`, styles in `style.css`.

- **The whole folder and filter, virtualized:** an inner element as wide as the list carries only the thumbnails near the visible
  part (about 25-40 nodes for a 300-photo folder, tested; the measure that matters for thousands). Nodes are keyed by photo id and
  positioned absolutely; the strip is updated in place, not rebuilt on every navigation.
- **Scrolling:** mouse wheel and trackpad (a vertical wheel scrolls sideways), touch flick, scrollbar (`touch-action: pan-x`, so a
  sideways drag scrolls the strip and never the page or the photo). **Click or tap** any thumbnail jumps to it, also from far away.
- **Centered on the current photo** (smooth scroll on navigation, instant the first time), but not while the user has just used the
  wheel or touched the strip (900 ms), so it never fights a hand. Found by a test: the "never touched" timestamp started at 0,
  which counts as "just touched" during the first 900 ms after page load; it starts at -infinity now.
- **Thumbnail requests:** images load only after scrolling has paused for 120 ms and only for the view plus 2 either side; nodes that
  scroll away cancel their download and are removed. Sweeping through a 300-photo strip in 0.6 s issued fewer than 60 requests instead of
  about 300 (a folder of RAW files would otherwise queue that many renders on the server).
- **Phones:** shown as a slim strip (thumbnails 66x44 px, still 44 px tall to tap), outside the swipe area of the photo; the buttons
  stay on screen and there is no horizontal page scroll.
- **With filters and undo:** the strip follows the list: when a photo leaves the view the strip shortens and the current thumbnail moves;
  undo brings it back (`state.onPhotosChanged`, also fired when more pages of the folder arrive).
- Tests in `tests/e2e/test_ui.py` (300-photo folder): covers the whole folder with few nodes and jumps from the far end; centered after
  navigation and after `End`; hand scrolling does not change the photo or get scrolled back and a far thumbnail is clickable; fast
  scrolling requests few thumbnails; filter/leave/undo; phone strip, tap and swipe on the strip.
- Not done (was listed as optional): rating badges on the strip thumbnails.

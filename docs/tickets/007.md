## Result (2026-09-21)

Done when "a whole year can be browsed without missing images". With the whole library scanned ([048](048.md)) and the
new `tools/check_images.py` (read-only on the library, scratch thumbnail directory, a private copy of the database), the year
**2021** (6,108 Photos, 2,740 DNG originals, no thumbnails at all before) was sampled through the real app code:

- 40 random Photos, sizes Thumb and Medium: **40/40 images** for each, 0 failures (median Thumb 1.3 s and Medium 0.4 s cold from the NAS).
- 20 random DNG originals (what the `G` key shows), sizes Thumb, Medium and Huge: **20/20** for each, 0 failures (Huge median 0.2 s).
- Also earlier: the whole `2022/2022-01-ny` folder browsed in Chrome (see 027).

The remaining years' thumbnails are created the same way on demand. To re-check another year:
`python tools/check_images.py --year=2019 --sample=60 --raw_originals=20`.

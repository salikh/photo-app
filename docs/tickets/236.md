## Result

Every slider of the adjustment panel (RAW and JPG share it, 235) has a 36 px **−** and **+** button on
either side: one slider step per tap, repeating after a 450 ms hold, going through the slider's own
input handler (preview, dirty state). Pointer Events, so it works with touch; keyboard activation
works too. Test: `test_plus_minus_buttons_step_a_slider_by_one_step_and_repeat_when_held`.

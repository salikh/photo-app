### Automatic linking of same-name files (2026-09-21)

The automatic rule now groups same-directory, same-name (case-insensitive) files when they include at
least two of RAW, JPG/JPEG, TIF/TIFF, PNG. Original = first of RAW > JPG > TIF > PNG; a JPG beside a
RAW is `camera`, all others are `tuning`, `derived_from` the original, `link_source='auto'`. Extra
files of the same kind and other types (GIF, WebP, HEIC) stay separate; manual `unlink` still wins.
Existing lone Photos merge into the group and the original's Photo (with its rating) survives.
Ratings are written only to the original's and the camera JPG's sidecars (023), not to tunings.
On the real `2001` + `2026` scan the rule merges 28 files (27 TIFF, 1 PNG): 1161 -> 1133 Photos.
Tests in `tests/test_grouping.py`.

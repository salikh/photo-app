# 053: Decision: should a reject beat a newer sidecar that says 1 or 2 stars?

- Type: question (decision)
- Blocks: [021](021.md) (the reject rule only)
- Status: open
- Refs: [023](023.md) (survey and the newest-wins decision), [049](049.md) (darktable's default 1 star)

When a Photo's DNG and JPG sidecars disagree, the app shows the newest sidecar's rating (your decision
in 023). The whole-library survey found 87 Photos where one sidecar says **reject (-1)** and the other
something else: in 75 of them the newer sidecar wins and the reject is hidden:

| Sidecars (rated values) | Photos | Newest-wins shows reject | Shows the other value |
|---|---|---|---|
| -1 vs 1 | 82 | 8 | 74 |
| -1 vs 2 | 5 | 4 | 1 |

Example: `2018/2018-01-Yaita/K___0253`: `DNG.xmp` says -1, `JPG.xmp` says 1, the app shows 1.
A "1" that darktable wrote as its default (049) probably does not mean "I changed my mind".

## Options

1. Keep pure newest-wins. Simple and predictable; 75 rejects stay hidden until you rate the Photo
   again (a write syncs both sidecars).
2. A reject beats a newer non-reject when the other value is 0 or 1 (a probable default), otherwise
   newest wins. Shows most of the 74 hidden rejects; a deliberate un-reject to 1 star would be
   overridden until you rate it again.
3. A reject always wins over any other sidecar value until the Photo is rated again. Safest for
   culling (nothing marked "reject" disappears); a real change of mind needs one explicit rating.

Proposed: option 2. Nothing is implemented; the rule is in `photoapp/ratings.py` (`resolve`).

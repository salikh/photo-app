# 049: Decision: how to treat darktable's default 1-star rating

- Type: decision
- Status: done
- Refs: [022](022.md), [048](048.md); docs/reqs/xmp-survey.md

Measured on 565 darktable-written sidecars (`*.JPG.xmp` / `*.DNG.xmp`): **76% have `Rating="1"`**,
12% have 2 stars, 4% have 3 stars, 4% are rejected (-1), under 1% have 0. darktable's default
"initial rating" on import is 1 star, so most 1-star ratings are very likely not a choice. In the
UI this puts a `★1` badge on about three quarters of all photos and makes the `picked` filter
(rating > 0) and the `unrated` filter nearly useless.

The app currently shows XMP faithfully (a 1 is a 1), because it cannot tell a default 1 from a
deliberate one.

## Options

1. Keep as is. Faithful, simple; the grid stays noisy and the app's own 1-star is
   indistinguishable from darktable's default.
2. Display-only: hide the `★1` badge in the grid and let `picked` mean "rating >= 2" (a setting
   with a flag such as `--one_star_is_unrated`). The data on disk is never changed.
3. One-time data migration: rewrite all 1-star sidecars to 0 (unrated). Destructive to real 1-star
   ratings and touches ~15k files; not recommended without a backup.

Proposed default: option 2 as an opt-in flag, off until the user answers.

## Decision

- Consider 1 star as "undecided, keep for now", and 0 stars as "undecided, leaning to reject", -1 "decided reject".
  Accordingly, do not show one star on thumbnails. Instead, show '?' on rating=0, and show 'x' on rating=-1.
  Ratings 2-5 shown in standard way (2 to 5 stars).

## Decision and implementation (2026-09-21)

**Option 2: display-only, opt-in flag `--one_star_is_unrated` (default off).** With the flag on:

- the grid no longer shows a `★1` badge, and the loupe shows a 1-star Photo as unrated;
- the filters change: `unrated` = 0 or 1 star, `picked` = 2+ stars, `rated` = 2+ stars or reject;
- the UI never offers 1: no `1` button, key `1` means unrated, and swipe up from unrated goes to 2
  (swipe down from 2 goes to unrated, not 1);
- `GET /api/config` tells the UI; the API and the sidecars still carry the real rating (the files
  panel lists the raw sidecar values).

Tests: `tests/test_library_api.py` (filters), `tests/e2e/test_ui.py` (badge, filter, buttons, keys,
step logic in the browser). To use it: `./start.sh --one_star_is_unrated`.

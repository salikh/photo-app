## What's wanted

`AnimSmall` is what the loupe shows when the browser cannot play the original ("This browser cannot play
.avi video"), so it should represent the video: the full video if it is under 60 s, otherwise five 15 s
fragments from different parts of it. The hover preview (`AnimThumb`, 8 x 1 s) must not change. All
previously made `AnimSmall` files are to be invalidated.

## Design

- `ANIM_SPEC` now carries (long edge, fps, fragments, fragment seconds, "whole clip up to" seconds) per
  size. `AnimSmall`: 640 px, 15 fps, 5 x 15 s, whole up to 60 s. `AnimThumb` is unchanged
  (300 px, 12 fps, 8 x 1 s, whole up to 8 s; test pins its recipe and fragment starts).
- Fragments are centred in five equal slices of the clip. Between 60 s and 75 s five 15 s fragments would
  overlap, so they shrink to `duration / 5` and tile the clip (so such a clip is effectively shown whole).
- Invalidation: each size has a recipe string recorded as its `thumbs` row's `source`
  (`ANIM_RECIPES`: `AnimThumb` `anim-v1`, `AnimSmall` `anim-v2`). `video_thumbs.invalidate_stale()` deletes
  every animated thumbnail (row and file) whose recipe is not the current one; the populator runs it at
  the start of each full `enqueue_missing()` pass (startup and the load-adaptive worker), then its normal
  logic remakes what is missing. Changing a recipe in future is: edit the spec, bump its string.
- Cost (measured on the real library, scratch directory): 45 s clip -> 1.2 MB, 18 s to make; 199 s clip ->
  2.6 MB, 48 s. The full backlog of ~770 videos takes roughly 4-5 hours on the single low-priority worker
  now (it was ~1.7 h); `ANIM_TIMEOUT` is 900 s.

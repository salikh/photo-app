## What's wanted

"The rules for JPG+RAF should be similar to JPG+DNG": one Photo per pair, RAW as the original, the
JPG as its camera file; rating/reject/tags, sidecars (`DSCF1001.RAF.xmp` full-name style like
`K.DNG.xmp`), moves/renames/trash, thumbnails and exports behave identically.

## Design

Nothing is expected to need a code change — every rule keys on `fileinfo.is_raw`, which includes
`.raf`. This ticket is an audit with a test per behaviour (parametrised over `.dng`/`.raf`/`.pef`
where a DNG test exists), and a fix for anything the audit finds. Also scan the real sample
directory read-only into a scratch DB (`--xmp_dry_run`, scratch state dir) and check: 24 Photos
with 2 files each, the MOV on its own, RAF original, DSCF1006/1024 (smaller RAW, different black
level) included.

## Outcome

Audit complete; **no production-code change was needed** — every rule keys on `fileinfo.is_raw`,
which already includes `.raf`. `tests/test_raf_pairing.py` locks it in (parametrised RAF/raf/DNG):
one Photo per pair, RAW `original` + JPG `camera`; an extra second RAW of the same stem and a lone RAF
stay single; `X.RAF.xmp` sidecars are read, a rating/reject goes to both files' sidecars, and a new
rating creates `X.RAF.xmp` (full-name style); move and trash carry the RAF, its sidecar and the JPG.
A `REAL_RAF`-gated test checks `X.RAF.jpg` Thumb/Small from the embedded preview and a demosaiced
Small after a WB change (X-Trans through `rawpy.postprocess`).

Scratch scan of four real files from the sample directory (DSCF1001/1006/1024 pairs + the MOV, copied
to a scratch tree, never the real one): 3 pair-Photos (RAF original + JPG camera, all with 'X-H1' and
the shot time) + the MOV on its own; Thumb/Small/Medium/Huge of a RAF take 30-80 ms each (embedded
preview). Note the Photo's *representative* file is the camera JPG, so the grid/loupe already show
the full-size JPG for a pair; see 221 for the remaining case.
